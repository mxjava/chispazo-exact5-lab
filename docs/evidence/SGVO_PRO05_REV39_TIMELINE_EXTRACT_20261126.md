## Interpretive guardrails

1. The participation/cancellation signature and the winners-relation signature are different records and occur at different stages.
2. The first signature is specified as deposited before the draw, but Annex 1 starts by checking SICAL is Closed. It is therefore not a pre-close predictor input under the documented flow.
3. The MJF archival copy is described as a daily post-21:00 operation. Do not assign an exact post-draw timestamp without the actual log.
4. CS-11's prescribed distribution is complete: original to SVO and copies to SEGOB, DTT, and the Control Data operator. Actual per-contest delivery/receipt evidence is not in the public procedure.
5. The weighing strip is generated after the verified close in the main flow. Do not use the same contest's measurements as pre-close information.
6. This document map is a controlled transcription/paraphrase of the public PDF, not an original signed record or proof of a particular contest's execution.
