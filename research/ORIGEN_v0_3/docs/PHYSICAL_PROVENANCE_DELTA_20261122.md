## 2. Revalidated electronic-draw procedure

The current public Normateca lists **SGVO-PRO-05 — Procedimiento Sorteos Electrónicos**, revision **39**, last updated **20-Mar-2026**; the PDF cover also prints publication date 20-Mar-2026. Its legal basis names the Chispazo regulation, and its attachment matrix explicitly includes Chispazo.

Draw-specific requirements relevant to the evidence package:

1. **Per-draw weighing:** every draw requires weighing. The procedure specifies Smartballs at 4.25–5.25 g with maximum lightest-to-heaviest spread 0.30 g, or Ping Pong at 2.30–3.00 g with maximum spread 0.15 g. If a case fails, another case is selected and weighed again.
2. **Tira de Pesaje:** the operator weighs the spheres one by one, in numerical order, in the presence of the SEGOB inspector and the designated Lotería representatives. The record is signed by the sphere-weighing responsible person and reviewed/rubricated by the representatives named in activity 5.4: Subdirección General de Ventas y Operaciones, Dirección General, and the SEGOB inspector. The glossary separately names the institutional role as representative of the Dirección de Operación de Concursos, Juegos y Sorteos. This is a role-label discrepancy between the activity step and glossary; preserve both source labels and obtain authoritative clarification rather than silently treating them as identical. The strip is delivered to be attached to the draw act.
3. **Scale calibration:** scales used for sphere weighing must be calibrated at least once per year. This is a procedural frequency, not proof that the particular scale used for any target draw was in calibration.
4. **Pre-draw participation-file custody:** the electronic signature for the participation file is recorded on storage media before the draw; the IT representative hands the device to the SEGOB inspector, who deposits it in a safe accessible only to the Órgano Interno de Control. A public result page cannot substitute for the original file, signature, inspector receipt, safe-deposit record, or access trail.
5. **Act and mandatory Chispazo annexes:** the act must record place, date, time, names, roles, signatures, inspector identity/commission, and incidents. The Chispazo matrix lists the sales-close summary, CS-11 winning-numbers form, draw summary, and Tira de Pesaje as attachments.
6. **Room access and incident controls:** the draw room is restricted and participants must be recorded in CS-12. Events affecting results must be recorded in the act and CS-09 incident log. CS-18 is a risk-communication mechanism when a participant identifies a control weakness or potential risk; it is not asserted to be a mandatory per-draw form.
7. **Recording and retention:** the procedure requires recording from sphere weighing through the electronic draw; the edited recording is a documentary witness and forms part of the act. It also describes weekly delivery of reports and electronic signatures on storage media under an official letter for attachment to the acts.

These are requirements for the records to request and verify. They do **not** prove that any particular 22-Nov record was created, signed, preserved, or independently held.

## 3. Scope guard: do not import traditional-draw ball specifications

The separately listed **SGVO-PRO-34** concerns verification of wooden balls, boleo distribution and abacus assembly. Its scope expressly applies exclusively to **Sorteos Tradicionales** and excludes electronic draws. Its wooden-ball dimensions and tolerances must not be substituted for the electronic Chispazo controls in SGVO-PRO-05.
