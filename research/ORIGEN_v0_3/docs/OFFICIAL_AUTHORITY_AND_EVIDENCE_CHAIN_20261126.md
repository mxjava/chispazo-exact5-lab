## 2. Legal authority of the result

The official **Reglamento del Concurso Chispazo**, whose PDF states its last reform was published 7-Mar-2012, provides:

- Article 8: the official winning results are those recorded in the Acta referred to by Article 15. The article separately labels terminal-emitted results as informational only.
- Article 15: after the ceremony and identification of winning combinations, an Acta is prepared recording the draw background, results and incidents; it is prepared with the SEGOB-appointed inspector and the officials identified by the regulation, who intervene and sign it.

## 4. Physical controls and primary records

SGVO-PRO-05 Rev.39 policy 6 specifies per-draw sphere weighing and these permitted values:

| Sphere type | Minimum weight | Maximum weight | Maximum set spread |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Smartballs | 4.25 g | 5.25 g | 0.30 g |
| Ping Pong | 2.30 g | 3.00 g | 0.15 g |

Policy 25 requires annual calibration of the scales used for sphere weighing. Policy 24 separately covers preventive/corrective maintenance of draw equipment.

The procedure identifies these four Chispazo Acta attachments in policy 11:
1. Sumario de Sorteo (cierre de venta).
2. Números Ganadores de los Sorteos CS-11.
3. Sumario de Sorteo.
4. Tira de Pesaje.

The Acta must identify place, date, event time, commissioned officials' names/roles/signatures, the SEGOB inspector's name/signature/commission-officio number, and incidents when present. It carries the Chispazo winner-relation report **CLSBR140**.

### CS-11 and weighing-strip sequence

The activity sequence in the current procedure says:
- Spheres are weighed one by one and in numerical order; the weighing strip records the values.
- The weighing responsible verifies each sphere against the policy parameters.
- The responsible person signs the weighing strip; it is reviewed/rubricated by the representative of the Subdirección General de Ventas y Operaciones, the SEGOB inspector, and the Dirección General.
- The signed strip is attached to the Acta.
- The sphere numbers captured in the machine displays are checked against CS-11; the numbers are read aloud before the Lotería Nacional representatives and SEGOB inspector.
- CS-11 is signed by the designated participants and the winning numbers are entered into the SICAL/selection systems in controlled repeated steps.

### Participation and custody controls

- Policy 8: before the draw, the electronic signature of the participation file is recorded to storage media; the IT representative hands it to the SEGOB inspector, who deposits it in the security box accessible only to the Órgano Interno de Control.
- The procedure identifies CS-12 as the room-attendance register for participants in the electronic draw.
- For nocturnal 21:00 draws, the annex describes validation of participation/cancellation files and signatures; after the 21:00 close, the MJF transaction master file and its printed electronic-signature form are also deposited in the security box.
- Policy 30 covers recording from sphere weighing through the draw and retention of the recording as documentary evidence.

- Nightly MJF file and printed electronic-signature form.

- Full weighing-to-draw recording and the edited draw recording retained with the Acta.
- SEGOB/OIC deposit record and independent custody events for each artifact.

The list separates verbatim policy-11 attachments from other procedure controls/supporting records; it does not relabel every control as an Acta attachment.
