## 2. Regulatory facts relevant to provenance

The compiled regulation makes several distinctions that ORIGEN must preserve:

- **Article 8:** the official winning results are those recorded in the act referenced in Article 15. Website and result-mask publications are expressly informational; if they differ from the official result, the act prevails.
- **Article 9(I):** the Chispazo ceremony uses one urn containing 28 spheres described as similar in all their characteristics, numbered 1 through 28.
- **Article 9(II)-(III):** the sales close and creation of the participation file occur in the presence of the specified institutional representatives; the file receives an electronic signature for integrity, is delivered to the SEGOB inspector, and is deposited in a safe accessible only to the Internal Control Body.
- **Article 9(IV):** five spheres are drawn one by one and at random in the presence of the named officials.
- **Article 15:** after the ceremony and identification of winning combinations, an act records the background, results and any incident; the SEGOB inspector and the named officials participate and sign it.
- **Article 15 bis:** a narrow force-majeure exception exists for inspector absence where a notary cannot be engaged; the designated institutional representative then performs the inspector's functions and delivers the act to the authority within three business days.

The text says the 28 spheres must be similar in their characteristics. The cited regulation itself does **not** provide a quantitative Chispazo sphere-mass tolerance or a full calibration/measurement protocol. Do not import tolerances from another lottery or another draw's regulation.

### Current operational procedure — SGVO-PRO-05, revision 39

A separate, currently published Lotería Nacional procedure adds operational controls for electronic draws:

- The public Normateca lists **PROCEDIMIENTO SORTEOS ELECTRÓNICOS (SGVO-PRO-05)**, revision **39**, last updated **20-Mar-2026**. The PDF itself identifies publication on 20-Mar-2026.
- Policy 6 requires weighing the spheres for **each draw**. It specifies:
  - **Smartballs:** each sphere 4.25–5.25 g; maximum difference between the lightest and heaviest sphere 0.30 g.
  - **Ping Pong:** each sphere 2.30–3.00 g; maximum difference 0.15 g.
  - If a case fails, another sphere case is selected and weighed again.
- Policy 7 names the SEGOB inspector, a General Directorate representative and a Sales/Operations representative as responsible for checking the sale close, weighing, loading, draw and entry of winning numbers; IT also checks the sale close, draw and result entry.
- Policy 8 says the electronic signature of the participation file is recorded to storage **before the draw**; the IT representative hands the device to the SEGOB inspector, who deposits it in a safe accessible only to the Internal Control Body.
- Policy 11 requires an act with place/date/time, names, roles and signatures of the commissioned officials and SEGOB inspector, plus incidents. Its Chispazo attachment matrix includes the sales-close summary, CS-11 winning numbers, draw summary and weighing strip.
- Policy 12 describes weekly DVD delivery of the reports and electronic signature of the winners report, under an official letter, for attachment to the acts.
- Policies 19 and 21 describe restricted draw-room access, a CS-12 attendance record and authorized access roles, including Internal Control Body staff retrieving information from the safe.

**Important scope limit:** these are procedural requirements and nominal acceptance ranges, not evidence of which sphere type was used in a particular Chispazo draw or the actual measured values for that draw. The original signed weighing strip, sphere-set identity, instrument/calibration data, act and custody records are still required. The procedure does not itself supply those draw-specific artifacts.

The source URLs are locators, not immutable evidence objects. The public procedure, result page and mask index are not substitutes for the signed act, original pre-draw participation file or measured sphere records.

Consequently, this observation supplies **no authenticated physical evidence** and no prospective evidence eligible for scoring. Do not append either contest to canonical history through this note; do not set pre-close, OOS, custody, score, or eligibility flags to true.

## 5. Minimum evidence request for a defensible physical audit

For each separately identified draw (12387 De las Tres and 12388 Clásico), request and preserve, as distinct immutable objects:

1. The complete Article 15 act, including all pages, signatures, inspector identity/authority and incident annexes.
2. The Article 9 signed participation-file manifest and cryptographic digest, the inspector's receipt, and the documented safe deposit/access chain.
3. The equipment and urn identifiers used for that draw, with setup, inspection, maintenance and changeover records.
4. The 28-sphere inventory and individual identifiers; pre-draw and post-draw measurements, instrument identity, calibration certificate, resolution, operator, witnesses and measurement timestamps.
5. The applicable approved technical procedure defining what “similar characteristics” means operationally, including any approved mass/diameter/surface tolerances. Do not substitute a procedure from another lottery.
6. Original audiovisual recording and official event log, where retained, with acquisition metadata and independent custody.
7. Original source bytes, byte lengths and SHA-256 digests computed at intake, plus independent timestamp and receipt evidence.

Keep result outcome records separate from physical-measurement records. A published combination cannot prove the physical state, identity, weight or calibration of the spheres that produced it.

### Additional retrieval hooks from the published SGVO-PRO-05 revision 39

The public procedure PDF identifies **SGVO-PRO-05, revisión 39**; its change-control table dates revision 39 to **March 2026**. Its Plan de Calidad supplies concrete record identifiers for a draw-specific request:

- **CS-02**: daily inspection of draw equipment before each draw; the quality plan says a nonconforming component or scale is replaced.
- **Tira de pesaje**: the quality plan requires checking each sphere against the applicable mass range and maximum lightest-to-heaviest spread. The record must be obtained with the sphere-case identity and all 28 measurements, not reconstructed from the published winning numbers.
- **CS-11**: the glossary says it records date, draw name/number/time, selected case, results, and names/signatures of the preparer, verifier, certifier and General Directorate representative. The quality plan also requires comparing the drawn spheres with the numbers entered on CS-11.
- **Policy 10 timing windows**: for De las Tres, inspector appointment 15:00, tolerance 15:05 and escalation limit 16:00; for nocturnal draws, 21:00, 21:05 and 22:00. The procedure describes a replacement-inspector path if needed. These are procedural windows, not proof that a specific inspector attended.
- **Policy 15 delayed-act exception**: if an act cannot be printed, it may be signed the next business day, with a maximum of five business days, only if results are not compromised; the Sales/Operations representative must record the issue in the Bitácora. Therefore an unsigned act immediately after a draw is a pending retrieval item, not by itself proof of noncompliance.

These controls improve the evidence request but do not provide any draw-specific CS-02, weighing strip, CS-11, inspector commission, act, or custody record. Obtain originals or certified copies and verify their issuing authority, signatures, timestamps, and chain of custody. Do not infer sphere type, measurements, attendance or equipment identity from the public mask.
