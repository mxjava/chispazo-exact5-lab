# Official physical-procedure scope audit — 2026-11-27

Status: **official procedure evidence only; no draw-specific physical records obtained**.

This note corrects the scope of two Normateca documents and records what they do—and do not—establish for the Chispazo Clásico physical-evidence line.

## 1. Modality boundary

Lotería Nacional's Chispazo product page identifies two daily products: **Chispazo de las Tres at 15:00** and **Chispazo Clásico at 21:00**. They must not be combined into one contest sequence or one physical-evidence sample.

Source: https://www.loterianacional.gob.mx/Chispazo/Chispazo

## 2. SGVO-PRO-05 Rev. 39 — applicable electronic-draw procedure

Official PDF:
https://www.loterianacional.gob.mx/Documentos/NormatecaInterna/2014/SUSTANTIVAS/VIGENTES/sgvo_pro_05.pdf

The document identifies itself as **SGVO-PRO-05, Procedimiento Sorteos Electrónicos, revision 39** (PDF pages 1–2).

Relevant controls:

- Policy 6 says sphere weighing is performed for **each draw**. It lists two possible sphere types and their acceptance ranges:
  - Smartballs: 4.25–5.25 g per sphere; maximum difference between the lightest and heaviest sphere 0.30 g.
  - Ping Pong: 2.30–3.00 g per sphere; maximum difference 0.15 g.
- Policy 9 identifies the automatic sales-close times, including 15:00 for De las Tres and 21:00 for the night draw.
- Activity 4.1 verifies and prints the automatic sales-close record with the contest date, time, contest number and sales status.
- Activity 5.1 then starts weighing the spheres one by one in numerical order, in the presence of the SEGOB inspector and Lotería representatives. The weighing strip is the named record.
- Activities 5.4–5.5 require signatures/rubrics by the sphere-weighing representative, the Subdirección General de Ventas y Operaciones representative, the SEGOB inspector and the Dirección General representative; the strip is delivered to be attached to the corresponding Acta.
- Activity 5.6 moves the spheres into the machine feeders. Activity 6.1 checks in front of the machines that the deposited spheres correspond to the draw.
- Policy 30 states that recordings from sphere weighing through the electronic draw are documentary witnesses for later clarification; the edited draw-only recording forms part of the Acta.

**Timing consequence:** the written sequence places sales close (activity 4) before the weighing process (activity 5). Same-draw weights therefore cannot be used as pre-close predictors for that same draw. A one-Classic-lag experiment remains only a proposal and still needs proof that the exact same physical spheres, number mapping, set, machine and feeder geometry persist into the next Classic draw before that target's sales close.

**Measurement limitation:** the policy's acceptable ranges are operating tolerances, not the 28 actual individual measurements for a draw. They do not identify which sphere type was used in a particular Chispazo draw, provide the actual weighing strip, or independently calibrate the V317 Plackett–Luce beta parameter.

## 3. SGVO-PRO-34 Rev. 01 — not applicable to Chispazo

Official PDF:
https://www.loterianacional.gob.mx/Documentos/NormatecaInterna/2014/SUSTANTIVAS/VIGENTES/sgvo_pro_34.pdf

The PDF identifies SGVO-PRO-34, revision 01, as a procedure for checking **wooden balls and ábacos**. Its scope section expressly says it applies exclusively to the boleo and ábacos used in **Sorteos Tradicionales** and excludes the activities during the draw itself.

Therefore, despite its title mentioning weight and diameter, SGVO-PRO-34 must **not** be used as the operating procedure, metrology specification, or evidence source for Chispazo's electronic draws. Its wooden-ball tolerances and sampling plan are not transferable to Chispazo's Smartballs/Ping Pong process.

The Normateca's current substantive-norm index lists SGVO-PRO-34 as a published internal norm, but its explicit scope restriction remains controlling for this project:
https://www.loterianacional.gob.mx/Normateca/Sustantivas?id=2

## 4. Evidence that remains unavailable

No draw-specific original physical records were obtained in this audit. In particular, there are no authenticated originals for:

- the 28 individual readings on the Chispazo weighing strip;
- the exact sphere type, case/set ID and 28 physical sphere IDs;
- the sphere-ID-to-printed-number mapping register and its validity interval;
- the scale ID and valid calibration certificate;
- the exact machine and feeder assignment for the source and next Classic draws;
- the source/target continuity record made before the target's sales close;
- the signed Acta and the weighing strip attached to it;
- the original weighing-to-draw recording and independent custody/retention evidence.

Public Normateca procedure PDFs establish written controls, not that a particular draw followed them or which physical objects were used. No PDF text extraction, screenshot, or public result mask is substituted for an original signed draw record.

## 5. ORIGEN disposition

1. Use SGVO-PRO-05 Rev. 39 as the relevant public procedure for the electronic-draw workflow, while preserving the 15:00 De las Tres / 21:00 Clásico distinction.
2. Exclude SGVO-PRO-34 from Chispazo model calibration and evidence claims because it is limited to traditional wooden-ball draws.
3. Keep V317.1 **BLOCKED** until draw-specific original weighing strips, mapping, equipment identity, calibration, pre-close continuity and independent custody are supplied and authenticated.
4. Do not infer sphere type, individual masses, physical continuity, or beta from procedure-level tolerance ranges.
5. V294 remains frozen; canonical history remains read-only; production remains **BLOCKED / RESEARCH_ONLY_NO_EDGE**.
