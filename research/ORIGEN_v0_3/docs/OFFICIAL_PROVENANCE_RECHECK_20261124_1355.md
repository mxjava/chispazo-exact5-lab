Relevant requirements visible in the official PDF:

- Policy 6: sphere weighing is performed for every draw; it specifies distinct limits for Smartballs and Ping Pong spheres.
- Policy 7: the SEGOB inspector, Dirección General representative and Subdirección General de Ventas y Operaciones representative verify closing, weighing, loading and draw operations; IT also verifies closing and SICAL result entry.
- Policy 8: the electronic signature of participation files is recorded before the draw and delivered to the SEGOB inspector, who deposits the device in the OIC-controlled safe.
- Policy 9: automatic sales close is 15:00 for *sorteos de las Tres* and 21:00 for *sorteos Nocturnos*. The Chispazo product page supplies the product-specific mapping: De las Tres at 15:00 and Clásico at 21:00.
- Policy 11 and its Chispazo annex matrix: the Acta file includes the sales-close summary, CS-11 winning-number report, post-result summary and weighing strip.
- Activity 5.1: spheres are weighed one by one in numerical order in the presence of the SEGOB inspector and the named Lotería Nacional representatives. Activities 5.4–5.5 require the weighing responsible person to sign the strip, obtain the representatives' rubrics, and attach it to the Acta.
- The glossary defines the weighing strip as signed by four roles: weighing responsible person, representative of the Dirección de Operación de Concursos, Juegos y Sorteos, representative of the Dirección General, and SEGOB inspector.
- Policy 12 requires the daily reports and electronic winner-report signature to be written to a weekly DVD and attached to the Acts for safekeeping.
